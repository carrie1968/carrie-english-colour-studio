A little about me

Good colour choices begin with listening.

I’m Carrie, the colour consultant behind Carrie English Colour Studio. I believe the best interiors aren’t about following every trend — they’re about understanding your home and choosing colours that support the way you want it to feel.

With professional colour-system training and a thoughtful eye for undertones, I help make paint decisions feel less overwhelming and much more personal.

Based in Burlington, Ontario, serving homeowners in the surrounding area. Virtual consultations may also be possible.
